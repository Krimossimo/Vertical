Features

Everything runs on your PC.

No upload, no account, no paid API. Your streams never leave your computer.

Smart local analysis

Whisper transcribes, audio analysis spots screams and laughs, AI recognises fights, deaths and wins on screen.

You stay in control

Preview every moment, adjust start and end, accept or reject. Keyboard shortcuts make it fast.

9:16 smart crop

Action-tracking crop, fixed or tracking facecam on top of the game, and a live phone preview.

Editable subtitles

Word-accurate timing, highlighted spoken word, custom colours and position. Fix, add or remove words.

Retention boosters

Hook title, progress bar and watermark, with GPU (NVENC) export and normalised loudness.

Ready to publish

Titles, descriptions and hashtags for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram generated with every Short.

Get started

Installed in three steps.

The installer sets up everything inside one folder. Nothing is installed elsewhere on your PC.

Download & unzip

Download Vertical-Setup.zip and extract it where you want to keep Vertical, for example D:\Vertical.

Run Installer.bat

Double-click Installer.bat. It checks what you already have and downloads the rest (Python, FFmpeg, AI models). It can take a few minutes.

Open Vertical.exe

Launch Vertical.exe or the desktop shortcut, pick a VOD and let it find your best moments.

Windows SmartScreen may show a warning the first time because the app is not code-signed. Click “More info” then “Run anyway”.

Recommended setup

  • Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
  • NVIDIA GPU (RTX 3060 or better) for fast analysis and export
  • 16 GB of RAM
  • ~8 GB of free disk space for the AI models

What you get

  • Up to 10 Shorts per 2-hour VOD, in about 15 minutes
  • 1080×1920 MP4, H.264, loudness-normalised
  • English and French interface
  • Works without a GPU (slower) and without internet after install
FAQ

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no paid API, no watermark added by default. If you like it, you can support the project on Ko-fi.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is analysed and rendered on your computer. Internet is only needed once to download the AI models.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet — Vertical is built for Windows.

Something is not working

Run Lancer Vertical.bat to see the console, or open an issue on GitHub with the console text.
